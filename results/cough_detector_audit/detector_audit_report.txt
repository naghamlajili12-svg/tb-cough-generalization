3. KEY SIGNAL PROCESSING FINDINGS
------------------------------------------------------------------------------
A. Microphone Sensitivity & Gain Discrepancies:
   - Smartphone microphones (Phone A, B, C) feature aggressive onboard Automatic Gain
     Control (AGC) and varying noise floors, whereas the Audio-Recorder preserves raw linear dynamics.
   - Fixed absolute thresholding fails across hardware. Relative/adaptive thresholding
     is mandatory.

B. MAD vs Relative dB Comparison:
   - Median + k*MAD adapts to the instantaneous baseline noise of each recording session.
   - k=6.0 provides the optimal balance between filtering handling noise / background chatter
     and capturing genuine cough explosive phases.
   - Relative dB above noise floor (NF + 20dB) is also competitive, but slightly more prone
     to over-detection in rooms with fluctuating background speech.

C. Listening-Based Validation:
   - Audio events identified by MAD k=6 correspond to real human coughs (explosive phase duration
     120-350 ms, total duration < 700 ms).
   - Highpass filtering at 80 Hz successfully removed mic handling thumps.
